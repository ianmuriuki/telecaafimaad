Data Privacy & DHIS2 Policy

TeleCaafimaad Banadir Pilot · Effective January 2026

1. Data We Collect

TeleCaafimaad collects the minimum patient data necessary to deliver clinical consultation services. All data is stored locally in your browser's localStorage during the pilot phase and is not transmitted to any external server except for the anonymised DHIS2 aggregate push described in Section 4.

2. How Data Is Stored

During the Banadir pilot, all patient and clinical data is stored exclusively in the browser's localStorage on the facility device. There is no cloud database, no external API, and no server-side session. Data keys are prefixed tc_.

  • tc_patients · registered patient demographics
  • tc_cases · consultation intake and clinical response
  • tc_referrals · specialist referral records
  • tc_documents · document metadata (filenames only; actual files are not uploaded in the pilot)
  • tc_dhis2_log · anonymised aggregate push log
  • tc_donations · donor contribution records
  • tc_facilities, tc_doctors, tc_specialists, tc_availability, tc_sessions · platform operational data

Full production deployment would move patient data to an encrypted server hosted within Somalia's national health data infrastructure in compliance with the Ministry of Health data governance framework.

3. DHIS2 Data Flow

After a consultation is closed, TeleCaafimaad simulates a push to Somalia's national DHIS2 instance (hmis.moh.gov.so/api/tracker). The following data is included in each tracker event:

1
Case closed by doctor
Doctor submits diagnosis and pathway. Case status changes to "closed" in localStorage.
↓
2
Anonymisation layer
Patient name and NID are stripped. Only district, sex, age band, diagnosis code, and pathway are included.
↓
3
DHIS2 tracker push
A TEI (Tracked Entity Instance) is created or updated on the national HMIS. HTTP 200 confirms success. Failed pushes are retried up to 3 times.
↓
4
Log entry created
Status (Success / Failed / Pending), HTTP code, and timestamp are stored in tc_dhis2_log and visible in the Admin DHIS2 Sync tab.

Data pushed to DHIS2 does not include: patient name, phone number, national ID, or any direct identifier. Only aggregate and pseudo-anonymised clinical indicators are transmitted.

5. Data Ownership

Patient data belongs to the patient and to the Somali Ministry of Health. AfyaMeet Limited, as the platform operator, is a data processor (not a data controller) and holds no proprietary rights over any clinical or demographic information collected through TeleCaafimaad.

In full production, data governance agreements will be signed with:

  • Somalia Federal Ministry of Health · primary data controller
  • Regional health authorities · secondary controllers for their jurisdictions
  • Participating facilities · data processors at point of collection
  • Diaspora specialist networks · data processing addendums limiting use to the specific referral context

6. Patient Rights

  • Right to know: Patients are informed at registration what data is collected and how it is used.
  • Right to access: A patient may request a copy of their consultation history from the facility worker at any time.
  • Right to correct: Errors in demographic data can be corrected by a verified facility worker.
  • Right to withdraw consent: A patient may withdraw consent for data collection at any point before a consultation is submitted. Withdrawal does not affect care delivery.
  • Right to delete: During the pilot, patients may request deletion of their record from the facility. Post-pilot, deletion requests will follow the MoH data retention policy (minimum 7 years for clinical records).
  • Right to not share with DHIS2: Patients may opt out of the anonymised DHIS2 push by informing the facility worker before consultation submission. This does not affect care quality.
