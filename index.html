01

Shaqaalaha Xarunta

Facility Worker

Health facilities are the first point of contact between patients and the TeleCaafimaad platform. Trained facility workers register patients, initiate consultations on their behalf, upload medical records, and manage referrals.

This interface is designed for staff at public health units, regional hospitals, and NGO-supported facilities across Somalia's pilot districts.

Dhakhtar Maxalliga

Local Doctor

Local physicians at onboarded facilities use this portal to manage their patient case queue, respond to consultations, request specialist input on complex cases, and issue structured referrals.

Doctors can initiate video consultations and attach clinical documents when referring patients to diaspora or urban specialists.

Demo login — ID: DR-001 · Password: doctor2026

03

Takhasuska Diaspora

Diaspora Specialist

Somali diaspora physicians and international specialists review referred cases, provide second opinions, and collaborate with local doctors remotely.

The platform matches specialists by specialty and language. All clinical records and documents shared by the referring doctor are available before the specialist responds.

Administration

The administration portal serves Ministry of Health officials, facility administrators, and donor partners. It provides a live donor dashboard, DHIS2 sync monitoring, facility registry with interactive map, doctor and specialist registries, and full case log with exportable reports.

Private Patient

Patients with direct internet access and ability to pay can book consultations independently through the private patient portal.

This mirrors the AfyaMeet Kenya model adapted for Somalia, with payment via EVC Plus, ZAAD, Sahal, or international card.
