Support Somalia's
Digital Health Infrastructure

Every contribution directly funds consultations for patients who would otherwise have no access to a doctor. TeleCaafimaad is a non-commercial pilot. 100% of donations go to platform operations and clinical staff.

Giving Tiers

$25
Single Consultation

Covers one full teleconsultation: intake, clinical review, prescription and DHIS2 reporting, for one patient at a Banadir facility.

$100
Facility Month

Keeps a single health facility connected to TeleCaafimaad for one month, including bandwidth, SMS and platform costs.

$500
Pilot Supporter

Sponsors a specialist slot for three months: a diaspora physician committing to respond to referrals from Somali local doctors.

$2,500
District Partner

Extends the pilot to one new district outside Banadir: facility onboarding, doctor training, and six months of full operations.

Make a Donation

This is a demonstration pilot. No real payment is processed. Submitted amounts are recorded in the demo database only.
